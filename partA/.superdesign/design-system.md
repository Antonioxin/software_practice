# WEMOVE Identity Design System

The Role A UI is a refined, warm, product-led extension of the captured WEMOVE site. It should feel like a quiet wooden workshop translated into a modern service portal: generous paper surfaces, precise dark-brown typography, thin rules, tactile wood accents, and calm editorial spacing.

Use the exact captured WEMOVE logo in every logo position. Public authentication pages use an asymmetric split with a photographic/product texture panel and a focused form. Account pages keep the public header. Admin pages are denser but retain the same type, colors, radius, and feedback language. Avoid purple gradients, generic SaaS cards, excessive shadows, bubbly children's styling, and decorative UI that competes with form clarity.

Accessibility is structural: visible labels, 44px controls, keyboard focus rings, error summaries plus field errors, text-supported status color, and reduced-motion handling. Responsive targets are 390px, 768px, and 1440px.
